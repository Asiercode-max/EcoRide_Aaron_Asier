# Frequently asked questions

The six questions people ask most often when they start using EcoRide or join the project.

???+ question "Do I need an account to see the stations?"
    No. The map and bike availability can be checked without logging in. Reservations, however, do require a verified account, because a reservation has to be assigned to a person.

???+ question "How much does EcoRide cost?"
    The planned model is a small monthly fee plus a per-minute charge after the first half hour, which is free. Final prices are not settled and are outside the scope of this academic project.

???+ question "What if the bike is broken when I reach the station?"
    From the bike details screen you can mark it as **broken**. The reservation is cancelled at no cost and the bike stays out of service until technical staff inspect it.

???+ question "Can I leave the bike at a different station?"
    Yes, as long as that station has free docks. The cost is based on usage time, not on the route, so origin and destination can differ.

???+ question "How is the CO₂ saving calculated?"
    The bike trip is compared with the same journey in an average combustion car: 120 g of CO₂ per kilometre travelled. It is a rough estimate, not an official measurement.

???+ question "Does the app work offline?"
    No. The map and reservations need a connection because they depend on the API. The history can be browsed in read-only mode using the last copy stored on the phone.

!!! note "Still have questions?"
    Check the [user manual](manual-usuari.en.md) for the full journey or the [API reference](api.en.md) if you need technical details.
